## Methodology

- Source: `market.ticks` for NIFTY 50 index token `256265`.
- Session used for opportunity benchmark: regular market `09:15-15:30 IST`; 09:00 pre-open ticks are excluded from swing opportunity.
- Candle construction: 1-minute OHLC from raw ticks, using first/last tick in each minute for open/close.
- Swing detection: deterministic close-based zig-zag on 1-minute closes with a `30 point` reversal threshold.
- Directional opportunity: sum of absolute points across all confirmed swing legs.
- Bias regime and opportunity shape are intentionally separate. A day can be `STRONG_BEAR_CLOSE_LOD` while still having internal counter-trend legs.
- Data-quality skip: missing open/close coverage, missing regular minutes, or max tick gap greater than 5 seconds.
- Gap label: current regular-session open vs previous clean trading-day close within this benchmark set.
